# Four starred entries: necessary PI-degree divisibility, NOT closure

Assuming the tabulated groups are correct and the PI AS-regular hypotheses
of the ozone divisibility theorem apply, let `d = PIdeg(B)`.
Since `|Oz(B)| | d^2`, the p-adic inequality
`v_p(d) >= ceil(v_p(|Oz|)/2)` holds for every prime p.

| Example | Proposed order | Necessary PI-degree multiple |
|---|---:|---:|
| C* | 12 = 2^2*3 | 6 |
| Q* | 36 = 2^2*3^2 | 6 |
| R* | 36 = 2^2*3^2 | 6 |
| V* | 36 = 2^2*3^2 | 6 |

This is only a necessary arithmetic constraint. It does NOT prove
that any of the four specializations is PI, determine its center,
establish completeness of the listed groups, or compute the PI degree.
For C*, the table's h satisfies h^2=-1/3, while for the other three
h=omega, as recorded in the historical table.
