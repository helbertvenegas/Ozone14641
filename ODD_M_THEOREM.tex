\begin{theorem}[Invariant factors when $m$ is odd]\label{thm:odd-m-structure}
Let $B$ be a PI member of $\mathbb M,\mathbb N,\mathbb O,\mathbb P,\mathbb W$ or $\mathbb Z$, over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Put
$m=\operatorname{ord}(u^2)$ and $d=m'=\operatorname{ord}(v^2)$, and suppose that $m$ is odd. Write
\[
 A_d=C_{2d}\times C_{2d}\times C_2,\qquad
 B_d=C_{4d}\times C_{2d},\qquad
 D_d=C_{4d}\times C_{4d},\qquad
 E_d=C_{2d}\times C_{2d}.
\]
Then the following table gives $\operatorname{Oz}(B)$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
 & \operatorname{ord}(u)\text{ odd},\operatorname{ord}(v)\text{ odd}&
 \operatorname{ord}(u)\text{ odd},\operatorname{ord}(v)\text{ even}&
 \operatorname{ord}(u)\equiv2\pmod4,\operatorname{ord}(v)\text{ odd}&
 \operatorname{ord}(u)\equiv2\pmod4,\operatorname{ord}(v)\text{ even}\\\hline
 \mathbb M,\mathbb P & A_d & B_d & B_d & A_d\;(\operatorname{ord}(v)\equiv2\!\!\pmod4),\ B_d\;(4\mid\operatorname{ord}(v))\\
 \mathbb N & B_d & A_d\;(\operatorname{ord}(v)\equiv2\!\!\pmod4),\ B_d\;(4\mid\operatorname{ord}(v)) & A_d & B_d\\
 \mathbb O,\mathbb W,\mathbb Z & E_d & D_d & A_d & B_d
\end{array}
\]
In the first three rows $A_d$ and $B_d$ are the only two possibilities for groups of order $8d^2$ containing the diagonal subgroup $C_{2d}^2$ with quotient $C_2$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof strategy and exact certificate]
If $m$ is odd, Table~\ref{tab:split} yields
$\Gamma/\Gamma_K=C_{2d}^2$; hence $N_K=C_{2d}^2$.
For $\mathbb M,\mathbb N,\mathbb P$, Proposition~\ref{prop:MNP} yields an exact sequence
$1\to N_K\to\operatorname{Oz}(B)\to C_2\to1$.
If $T$ lifts the nontrivial element, the group is abelian, so the extension
splits precisely when $T^2\in 2N_K$. The class of $T^2$ in
$N_K/2N_K\cong C_2^2$ is computed from the double-swap constants
$(c_1,c_2;d_1,d_2)$ of Table~\ref{tab:act}. If $T=S\chi$ with
$\chi(e_1)=a e_1$, $\chi(e_2)=a\varepsilon_xe_2$,
$\chi(z_1)=bz_1$, $\chi(z_2)=b\varepsilon_yz_2$, then
\[
 T^2\big|_{V_e}=(c_1c_2)\varepsilon_xa^2 I,
 \qquad T^2\big|_{V_z}=(d_1d_2)\varepsilon_yb^2 I.
\]
Equality of the restrictions $T|_K$ and one of
$\operatorname{Ad}_e|_K$, $\operatorname{Ad}_z|_K$,
$\operatorname{Ad}_{ez}|_K$ yields equations for
$a^{2d}$ and $b^{2d}$. Substitution gives the first three rows.
The phase calculations, including the dependence on $\operatorname{ord}(v)$
modulo four, are independently enumerated with rational root-of-unity
exponents in the supplementary script \texttt{odd\_m\_analysis.py}.

For $\mathbb O,\mathbb W,\mathbb Z$, if $\operatorname{ord}(u)$ is even,
there are no double swaps in the ozone group. The diagonal group is an
extension of $N_K$ by $C_2$; the same square-class test gives $A_d$
for odd $\operatorname{ord}(v)$ and $B_d$ for even $\operatorname{ord}(v)$.
If $\operatorname{ord}(u)$ is odd and $\operatorname{ord}(v)$ is even,
$L=K$ and the quotient is $C_2^2$. Two independent cosets have square
classes forming a basis of $N_K/2N_K$. Hence the invariant factors are
$C_{4d}^2$.

If both $\operatorname{ord}(u)$ and $\operatorname{ord}(v)$ are odd,
$L=K(c)$ and the degree of $c$ given in the proof of
Proposition~\ref{prop:OWZ} adds an order-two relation to
$\Gamma/\Gamma_K$. This gives
$N_L\cong C_{2d}\times C_d$. The index-two quotient has a lift whose
square has trivial order-two component (as follows from the table of
restrictions on $K$). Because $d$ is odd, its square in the odd-order
part of $N_L$ can be eliminated by multiplying the lift by an element
of $N_L$. The extension therefore splits and yields
$N_L\times C_2\cong C_{2d}^2$.
\end{proof}
